Quick Answers

Common Questions

How long does a brand identity project take?

Typically 2–4 weeks depending on scope and feedback cycles. Rush projects can be accommodated with advance notice.

Do you work with international clients?

Absolutely! We serve clients across Bangladesh and internationally. All communication and file delivery is handled digitally.

What formats are the final files delivered in?

We deliver all source files (AI, PSD, Figma) plus print-ready PDFs and web-optimized formats (PNG, SVG, WebP).

How many revisions are included?

Starter packages include 2 rounds. Growth and Enterprise packages include unlimited revisions until you're 100% happy.
