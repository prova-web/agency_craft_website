Our Story

Passion, Purpose & Creativity

Founded in 2016, ArtCraft Studio has grown from a two-person passion project into Rangpur's most trusted creative design agency.

Who We Are

Creativity Is Our
Craft

ArtCraft Studio began in 2016 when two passionate designers in Rangpur decided local businesses deserved world-class creative work. What started in a small studio has grown into a 25-member powerhouse serving clients across Bangladesh and beyond.

We believe design is not decoration — it's communication. Every colour, typeface, and shape we choose is intentional, purposeful, and designed to tell your brand's story in the most compelling way possible.
